# Mortal Warcraft - Complete Feature List
## Comprehensive Overview of All Systems

**Realm 1: Mortal Sandbox** - Classless, skill-based, full-loot PvP zones

---

## Core Systems

### Progression
- **Classless System** - No classes, skill-based progression
- **Derived Level (1-25)** - Calculated from total skill points
- **Skill-Based Advancement** - Skills level through use (combat, gathering, crafting)
- **Attribute Caps** - Individual: 150, Total: 400 (proportional overflow)
- **Mentor System** - Scale down power for PvE content (free respecs until 200 skill points)
- **Mastery Trees** - Warlord, Guardian, Explorer trees
- **Build Presets & Loadouts** - Save and swap between build configurations

### Combat
- **Custom Formulas** - Hit chance, crit, damage, health, mana
- **Brace Mechanic** - 50% damage reduction on successful brace
- **Guard Counter** - Timed defensive responses (perfect block → empowered counter)
- **Material Multipliers** - Item quality affects damage
- **Weapon/Magic Skill Bonuses** - Convert skill points to AP/SP
- **Mana Regeneration** - Base + Spirit + Shrine bonuses
- **Spell & Ability Library** - Classless spell system (Core Universal, Learned Skills, Runes, Mastery Actives, Augments)
- **Tab-Target Hybrid** - WoW-style combat with Mortal timing mechanics

### Risk Zones
- **Green Zones** - Safe, no PvP, no loot drops
- **Yellow Zones** - Mid-risk, criminal flag on aggression, partial loot
- **Red Zones** - Full-loot PvP, teleports disabled
- **Zone Signage** - Visual/audio warnings on entry
- **Environmental Hazards** - Visual cues for danger
- **Northrend Cold Weather** - Cold weather mechanics in Northrend zones

### Economy
- **Regional Banking** - Separate bank storage per city/region
- **Market Stalls** - Player-owned vendor stalls with physical locations
- **Courier Contracts** - Player-driven item transport with risks
- **Caravans** - Trading caravans with movement physics
- **NPC Buy Orders** - Cities place buy orders for materials
- **Hot Zones** - Weekly rotating regional economic bonuses
- **Blessed Items** - Soft insurance (5 gold, 7 days, 3 charges)
- **Auction House Policy** - Strict anti-spam limits, commodity stack rules
- **Blueprint Originals/Copies (BPO/BPC)** - Crafting blueprint system

### Crafting
- **Material Lore** - Knowledge of materials affects quality
- **Workstation-Based** - Must use appropriate workstation tier
- **Procedural Quality** - Quality score determines item tier (Shoddy → Legendary)
- **Durability Decay** - Permanent durability loss on death/use
- **Failure & Break Chance** - Skill deficit, wrong workstation, low materials
- **Encumbrance** - Items have weight, affects movement speed
- **Refining Stations** - Material processing stations

### PvE
- **NPC Tier System** - 5 tiers (M-T1 through M-T5)
- **Public Dungeons (Delves)** - Open-world dungeons
- **Extraction Raids** - Raids with cursed artifacts requiring extraction
- **World Bosses** - Dynamic world boss system
- **Task Boards** - Procedurally generated tasks with dynamic rewards
- **Contracts** - Courier, caravan, escort contracts
- **Endless Contracts** - Defense & Survival wave-based content
- **Instance Tier Mapping** - Dynamic scaling based on tier

### PvP Systems
- **Notoriety System** - Track player kills, criminal/outlaw states
- **Bounty System** - Place bounties on outlaws, claimable by others
- **Anti-Zerg** - Damage reduction for outnumbered players
- **Crime System** - Criminal flag on aggression in Yellow zones
- **Outlaw Restrictions** - Outlaws restricted from Green zones
- **Friendly Fire in Red** - Friendly fire enabled in Red zones
- **Arena & Rating** - Arena system with ratings
- **PvP Vendors & Rewards** - Military Credits, PvP gear vendors

## Key Design Principles

1. **No XP System** - Skills level through use, no experience points
2. **Gold-Based Economy** - Gold and materials, not XP
3. **Risk vs. Reward** - Higher risk = higher rewards
4. **Full Loot PvP** - Red zones = full loot on death
5. **Classless** - Build your character through skills
6. **Player-Driven** - Economy, contracts, caravans driven by players
7. **Territory Control** - Guilds control territory and resources
8. **Living World** - Dynamic ecosystems, weather, seasons
9. **Infantry-First Combat** - Player skill > vehicle reliance
10. **Sandbox Identity** - Emergent gameplay through world systems
