### Topic: Healer Set – T1/T2 Itemization (“Sanctum Warden” / “Light of the Covenant”)

> This file defines concrete T1/T2 item blueprints for the **Healer** archetype:  
> a support-focused build that keeps groups alive in delves, sieges, Warfronts, and world events.

This aligns with:

- Stat model: INT + SPIRIT as primaries, with enough STA to survive focus,
- Armor: Cloth (with possible leather accessories if desired),
- Weapons: Staff, Mace + Tome/Offhand, etc.

We assume **6-piece sets** for T1 & T2:

- Helm, Chest, Legs, Gloves, Boots, Shoulders  
- Weapons/offhands are themed but not formal set pieces.

---

## 1. T1 Set – “Sanctum Warden”

**Fantasy:**  
The first responders and triage healers who stabilize allies during early delves and crises around fledgling shrines.

**Power Band:**  
- Roughly early dungeon/heroic-ready for group play,  
- Meant to be the **entry dedicated healer kit**.

### 1.1 Set Summary

- **Set Name:** Sanctum Warden
- **Slots:** Head, Chest, Legs, Hands, Feet, Shoulders
- **Armor Type:** Cloth
- **Weight Tag:** LIGHT
- **Primary Stats:** Intellect, Spirit
- **Secondary:** Bonus Healing/Spell Power, small Haste, Mana Regen effects
- **Rune Slots:** 1 healer rune slot on chest & legs.

### 1.2 Set Bonuses

- **2-piece:**  
  +Intellect, +Spirit, +Healing Power.

- **4-piece – “Warden’s Grace”**  
  Your **direct heals** on targets below 50% HP have a small chance to trigger *Warden’s Grace* on them for 4s:  
  - They take **X% reduced damage** (e.g. 5%).  
  (Internal CD per target.)

- **6-piece – “Sanctum Echo”**  
  Every time you cast a **single-target heal**, there is a small chance to create a **minor smart heal** that jumps to a nearby injured ally (within short range).  
  (Internal CD global.)

## 2. T2 Set – “Light of the Covenant”

**Fantasy:**  
Battlefield chaplains and covenant healers who stand in the thick of sieges and world-ending crises, radiating powerful restorative magic.

**Power Band:**  
- Strong group-healing gear for sieges, Warfronts, high-tier delves, and major events like Midnight Horde.

---

## 2.1 Set Summary

- **Set Name:** Light of the Covenant
- **Slots:** Head, Chest, Legs, Hands, Feet, Shoulders
- **Armor Type:** Cloth
- **Weight Tag:** LIGHT
- **Primary Stats:** High Intellect, high Spirit, moderate Stamina
- **Secondary:** Healing Power, Haste, crit for heals, regen.

### 2.2 Set Bonuses

- **2-piece:**  
  +High Intellect, +Spirit, +Healing Power.

- **4-piece – “Covenant Radiance”**  
  Casting a **big heal** (e.g. your highest-rank single-target or a designated spell) grants *Covenant Radiance* for 6s:  
  - Your next **AoE heal** heals for extra X% and leaves a small HoT.

- **6-piece – “Bulwark of Light”**  
  When **3 or more allies within range** are below 50% HP, your next AoE or chain heal also applies a **small absorb shield** to them.  
  (Internal CD: e.g. 20–30s.)

## 3. Acquisition & Progression (High-Level)

Per the global matrix:

- **T1: Sanctum Warden**
  - **Primary:** Public Delves with healing-heavy encounters (sanctuaries, infirmaries, escort delves).
  - **Secondary:** Healing Contracts (triage events, refugee aid, post-event recovery).
  - **Campaign:** Early sanctuary/clinic storyline grants guaranteed Sanctum Warden pieces.

- **T2: Light of the Covenant**
  - **Primary:** Stronghold sieges, Warfronts, and high-tier delves where healers are mandatory.
  - **Secondary:** World bosses & Midnight Horde events.
  - **Campaign:** Key covenant binding moment in the main story where you formally become a high-tier healer of a shrine or order.

Implementation:  
Assign visually appropriate priest/paladin-style WotLK models for cloth, repurposed as Mortal items, and wire them into healer-centric loot tables for the content above.
