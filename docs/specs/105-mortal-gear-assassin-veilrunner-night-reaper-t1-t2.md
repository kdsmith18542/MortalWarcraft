### Topic: Assassin Set – T1/T2 Itemization (“Veilrunner” / “Night Reaper”)

> This file defines concrete T1/T2 item blueprints for the **Assassin** archetype:  
> a stealthy, high-burst, single-target killer specializing in picks, flanks, and finishing low-HP enemies.

This aligns with:

- Stat model: AGI > STA, with small INT if needed,
- Armor: Leather (with some cloth accessories if desired),
- Weapons: Dual daggers/swords, or dagger + fist weapon.

We assume **6-piece sets** for T1 & T2:

- Helm, Chest, Legs, Gloves, Boots, Belt  
- Weapons are themed and recommended, but **not** formal set pieces (to maintain flexibility).

---

## 1. T1 Set – “Veilrunner”

**Fantasy:**  
A light-footed killer who lives on the edge of the fight, darting in from stealth, finishing wounded targets, and slipping away.

**Power Band:**  
- Equivalent to early “heroic 5-man / low-tier PvP” gear,  
- Intended as the **first dedicated assassin kit** for players who commit to stealth/burst gameplay.

### 1.1 Set Summary

- **Set Name:** Veilrunner
- **Slots:** Head, Chest, Legs, Hands, Feet, Belt
- **Armor Type:** Leather
- **Weight Tag:** LIGHT
- **Primary Stats:** Agility > Stamina
- **Secondary:** Crit, small Haste/Attack Speed, a bit of Hit/Expertise
- **Rune Slots:** 1 offensive rune slot on chest & legs.

### 1.2 Set Bonuses

- **2-piece:**  
  +Agility, +Crit Chance.

- **4-piece – “Veil Ambush”**  
  Exiting **Stealth** grants *Veil Ambush* for 3s:  
  - +X% Crit chance (e.g. 10–15%) on your first melee attack.  
  (Internal CD: 10s.)

- **6-piece – “Hunted Edge”**  
  Your melee attacks deal **+Y% increased damage** to targets below 30% HP  
  (e.g. 5–10%, tunable).  

> Numbers are placeholders; refine after live testing.

## 2. T2 Set – “Night Reaper”

**Fantasy:**  
An apex predator of the battlefield — strikes from shadows, chains kills, and applies bleeding wounds that are hard to heal through.

**Power Band:**  
- Equivalent to serious PvP / high-end delve gear,  
- Emphasizes **kill chaining** and **healing reduction** mechanics.

---

## 2.1 Set Summary

- **Set Name:** Night Reaper
- **Slots:** Head, Chest, Legs, Hands, Feet, Belt
- **Armor Type:** Leather
- **Weight Tag:** LIGHT
- **Primary Stats:** High Agility, medium Stamina
- **Secondary:** Crit, Haste, some Armor Pen / Attack Power depending on final stat model.

### 2.2 Set Bonuses

- **2-piece:**  
  +High Agility, +Crit Rating.

- **4-piece – “Shadow Rush”**  
  Getting a **Kill or Assist** grants *Shadow Rush* for 5s:  
  - +20% movement speed,  
  - +10% damage dealt.  
  (Internal CD: 15s; buff does not stack.)

- **6-piece – “Reaper’s Mark”**  
  Critical strikes from behind apply **Reaper’s Mark** for X seconds:  
  - A moderate bleed,  
  - And **reduces healing received** on the target by a small % (stacking to a cap across multiple applications/players).

## 3. Acquisition & Progression (High-Level)

This plugs into the global set matrix:

- **T1: Veilrunner**
  - **Primary:** Hellgates (T1) and small-group PvPvE.
  - **Secondary:** Bounty Hunter Contracts (Task Boards).
  - **Campaign:** Intro bounty arc grants 1–2 Veilrunner pieces.

- **T2: Night Reaper**
  - **Primary:** Hellgates (T2) in risky Red Zone entry points.
  - **Secondary:** High-value bounties and Cartel/Black Market events.
  - **Campaign:** Advanced outlaw/assassin storyline where you become a named hunter or feared killer.

Implementation detail:  
Assign WotLK leather assassin-looking models (e.g., rogue PvP sets) to these Mortal item entries and wire drops according to Hellgate & Contract specs.
