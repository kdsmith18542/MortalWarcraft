# Project Canvas: Mortal Warcraft Overhaul  
### File: 88-mortal-progression-era-map.md  
### Topic: Progression & Era Map – What Players Actually Do (Hours 0–100+)

> This document stitches together all core systems into a **temporal progression view**:  
> what players typically do in **Hours 0–2, 2–10, 10–50, and 50+**, and how WoW/EVE/Mortal/RuneScape/Rift/Elden systems blend.

---

## 1. Design Goals

1. Provide a **clear mental model** of “what now?” for players and designers.
2. Ensure there is **always something meaningful to do** at each stage:
   - Combat, economy, exploration, social.
3. Make sure early experiences **onboard players into the sandbox** painlessly:
   - While preserving the danger of Red Zones and full loot.
4. Give designers a reference when adding new content:
   - "Does this help 0–10? 10–50? 50+?"

## 2. Era 1 – Awakening (Hours 0–2)

**Location:**  
- Shipwreck Cove → Mainland Hub (Port Meridian / equivalent).

**Primary Goals:**

- Learn core controls and **Mortal-specific rules**.
- Introduce **Dynamic Level**, **Flasks**, **Risk Zones**, and **basic Tasks**.
- Get players to their **first archetype-adjacent choice** (weapon + stat direction).

### 2.1 Key Systems Touched

- Two-stage tutorial:
  - Shipwreck (survival/combat).
  - Mainland Hub (economy/logistics).
- Mentor system for **free respec** until 200 Skill Points.
- Flask/Crimson Phial basics.
- Simple Task Board.

### 2.2 Typical Activities

- Kill training dummies to see **skill gain** instead of XP.
- Gather simple resources, craft a basic weapon.
- Learn about:
  - Green / Yellow / Red Zones.
  - Shrine respawn (Shrine System).
  - Corpse chests and full-loot rules.
- Do first **Hub Contracts**:
  - Deliver crate across a safe zone.
  - Buy a backpack from a player stall.
  - Deposit reward in regional bank.

### 2.3 Milestones

- Acquire:
  - First weapon aligned with archetype (sword, staff, bow, etc.).
  - Basic armor set.
- Unlock:
  - Access to `/world` (post-onboarding).
  - First few Runes (starter weapon arts or spells).

Outcome: player leaves Era 1 knowing **how not to die instantly** and with a vague sense of “I’m going to be a X-type character.”

## 3. Era 2 – Finding a Lane (Hours 2–10)

**Location:**  
- Green/Yellow Zones around the hub (Elwynn/Barrens equivalents).
- Occasional forays near Yellow/Red borders.

**Primary Goals:**

- Let players **sample archetype lanes** via Tasks, Contracts, and item drops.
- Introduce:
  - **Task Boards** (Civic, Shrine, Frontier, Atlas, Cartel-lite),
  - **Public Dungeons** (low-tier),
  - First **Standing gains** with major factions.

### 3.1 Systems Touched

- Task Boards:
  - Civic: simple defense/patrol/wolf culling.
  - Shrine: healing/relief tasks, escorting pilgrims.
  - Frontier: scouting edges of Yellow zones.
  - Atlas: map-making, resource survey tasks.
  - Cartel-lite: low-stakes smuggling/black market intros.
- Standing system (Civic, Shrine, Atlas).
- First public dungeons (low difficulty).

### 3.2 Typical Activities

- Players start behaving as:
  - Linebreaker: bodyguard, frontline tasks.
  - Warden: minor healing/support Contracts.
  - Ranger: scouting and courier runs.
  - Magus: high-damage Contracts, anomaly poking.

- Join:
  - Small groups for early public dungeons.
  - Ad-hoc Warfront “skirmish” events (low-stakes).

### 3.3 Milestones

- Hit **Dynamic Level ~3–5** through skill points.
- Reach **Standing ~+10–20** in at least one faction.
- Assemble first **T1 gear pieces** from Contracts/public dungeons.

Outcome: Player has experimented with at least 1–2 lanes and has **natural affinity** toward one or two archetypes based on what they enjoy doing.

## 4. Era 3 – The First Commitments (Hours 10–50)

**Location:**

- Mix of Green/Yellow, regular dives into Red edges.
- First exposure to:
  - Full public dungeons in contested zones,
  - Small Red Zone expeditions,
  - Early Stronghold interactions.

**Primary Goals:**

- Get players into **guilds or stable social circles**.
- Expose them to:
  - **Full-loot PvP** (but not constantly),
  - **Strongholds & territory**,
  - **Shrine/Cartel/Frontier identity**.

### 4.1 Systems Touched

- Full Task Board spectrum with harder variants.
- Stronghold Sovereignty:
  - Control Points, minor sieges.
- Warfronts as serious content – full-loot battlegrounds.
- Black Market (Cartel Standing, fencing stolen goods).
- Atlas & anomaly systems at mid-tier difficulty.

### 4.2 Typical Activities

- **Linebreakers & Marshals:**
  - Tanking public dungeons, joining Warfronts, guarding Strongholds.
- **Wardens:**
  - Becoming essential in dungeon groups and Red excursions.
- **Magus / Occultists:**
  - Providing high-risk damage in delves and Warfronts.
- **Rangers / Pathfinders:**
  - Scouting routes, guiding caravans, mapping anomalies.
- **Cartel Brokers:**
  - Running higher-value trade routes, minor smuggling.

Players:

- Lose and replace their first **real** gear to full-loot deaths.
- Learn to use **insurance** / Soul Insurance properly.
- Develop habits: run Contracts → upgrade gear → reinvest → risk bigger runs.

### 4.3 Milestones

- Join a **guild** or become a regular in some group.
- Reach mid-tier standings: **+30–50** in chosen factions.
- Assemble most of a **T2 gear set** appropriate to archetype.
- Participate in:
  - First significant Stronghold defense/assault,
  - At least one Cursed Raid extraction run (even as a support role).

Outcome: Player is now “in the world” – integrated into its economy and conflict. Quitting now means walking away from social/faction identity, not just stats.

## 5. Era 4 – Realm Actors (Hours 50–100+)

**Location:**

- Regularly in Yellow/Red Zones.
- Deep public dungeons and rift content.
- Major Warfronts and Stronghold sieges.

**Primary Goals:**

- Turn experienced players into:
  - **Atlas-grade intel sources**,
  - **Stronghold leaders & guild officers**,
  - **Market movers & Cartel figures**,
  - **Shrine champions & heal anchors**.
- Ensure long-term progression is **horizontal & systemic**, not just vertical ilvl.

### 5.1 Systems Touched

- Full Stronghold Sovereignty loop:
  - Taxes, territory buffs, local banking and crafting benefits.
- High-tier Warfronts with meaningful resource shipments.
- World events:
  - Midnight Horde,
  - Zone Rifts and large invasions,
  - Hellgates, World Boss fragments.
- Atlas Portal & Mortal Atlas web portal integration:
  - Players use Atlas as a serious intel tool.

### 5.2 Typical Activities

- **Guild leaders / Marshals:**
  - Planning sieges, setting tax rates, scheduling Warfront pushes.
- **Atlas Pathfinders / Rangers:**
  - Constantly feeding the guild intel on enemy movements, bosses, anomalies.
- **Wardens:**
  - Leading healing squads, shrine defenses, ritual runs.
- **Magus / Occultists:**
  - Specializing in specific content lanes (Hellgates, Rifts, high-tier public delves).
- **Cartel Brokers:**
  - Running the Black Market, cornering regional goods, laundering high-value stolen gear.

### 5.3 Milestones

- Achieve **high standings** (60–100) with chosen core factions.
- Possess:
  - T2+ gear with meaningful Rune setups,
  - Good insurance habits,
  - A known role in at least one guild/coalition.
- Participate in:
  - Major world events,
  - Realm-shaping sieges.

Outcome: Player is not just “more powerful”; they are **structurally embedded** – with territory, reputation, enemies, and economic footprint.

## 6. Long-Term Loops & Alt Paths

Mortal should **not** force everyone into war leadership as “endgame.” Long-term players can:

1. **Be warlords** – living on Warfronts, sieges, and Red Zone conflict.
2. **Be industrialists** – running Stronghold industry, Cartel pipelines, crafting and markets.
3. **Be explorers** – anomaly hunters, Atlas intel providers.
4. **Be shrine guardians** – high-end support, event coordinators.
5. **Be rift/occult specialists** – chasing the hardest PvE anomalies and Hellgates.

Each lane uses **the same shared systems** (Standing, regional economy, full loot, Atlas, Shrines, Strongholds) with different emphasis.

## 7. System Checks: Blending Confirmations

This Era Map ensures the blend feels intentional:

- **WoW**:
  - Dungeons, raids, instanced content → folded into Contracts, delves, Cursed Raids.
- **EVE**:
  - Stronghold sovereignty, Standing, markets, hauling → expressed via Frontier, Cartel, Atlas, Civic factions.
- **Mortal Online**:
  - Full loot, local banks, harsh Red Zones → introduced gradually and made insurable.
- **RuneScape**:
  - Skilling, task boards, Contracts → always-available progression even when not raiding.
- **Rift**:
  - Zone invasions and Rifts → events layered into Yellow/Red zones and World Events.
- **Elden Ring**:
  - Flask system, guard counters, open progression → present from Era 1, but mastered in Era 2–3.

## 8. Implementation Notes

- No new core systems required; this doc **organizes** existing systems.
- Use this as:
  - A reference when adding new features (“which Era does this serve?”),
  - A basis for a **player-facing “What Now?” guide** on the Mortal Atlas web portal.
- MortalUI and the in-game tutorial can:
  - Surface Era-appropriate hints,
  - Suggest Contracts / boards that match current progression.

This document should live alongside the other specs as the **high-level player progression spine**, ensuring all new content slots cleanly into one or more Eras.
